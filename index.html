Our Mission

Recovery isn't just about staying sober — it's about rebuilding a life worth staying sober for.

"We provide the structure. You bring the willingness. Together, we build the life you're working toward."

Black Rock Recovery, LLC was founded to close the gap between clinical treatment and independent living. Early recovery is fragile — residents need more than a bed; they need structure, accountability, and a community that understands what they're carrying.

Our homes give residents room to practice the skills of daily life — routine, responsibility, honesty, and connection — while surrounded by peers walking the same path and staff who hold high, consistent standards.

  • Safety First

    Zero-tolerance substance policy, random testing, and a secure, drug-free environment for every resident.

  • Accountability

    House meetings, curfews, chores, and check-ins keep residents grounded and consistent.

  • Community

    Recovery happens in relationship. Residents support one another through shared structure and mutual respect.

  • Dignity

    Every resident is treated as a whole person — not defined by their addiction, but supported through it.

Our Program

What life at Black Rock Recovery looks like

A structured, phase-based approach designed to help residents move from early stabilization toward independent, sustainable recovery.

Structured Routine

Set wake times, curfews, chore rotations, and required house meetings build the daily discipline that supports long-term sobriety.

Peer Accountability

Residents support and hold one another accountable, guided by house leaders and staff who reinforce healthy boundaries.

Random Testing

Routine, unannounced drug and alcohol screening protects the integrity of the house and every resident's recovery.

Recovery Meetings

Residents are required to actively participate in 12-step or other recovery meetings, plus regular check-ins with a sponsor or mentor.

Life Skills & Employment

Support with job searches, budgeting, transportation, and reconnecting with family builds a practical path forward.

24/7 Support

On-call staff and house leadership are always reachable for guidance, accountability, or support during a hard moment.

House Guidelines

What we ask of every resident

These standards protect the recovery of everyone in the house. A complete residency agreement is reviewed and signed at move-in.

Zero-tolerance policy

No use of drugs or alcohol on or off premises. Violations may result in immediate discharge.

Curfew & check-ins

Nightly curfew and regular check-ins with house leadership keep everyone accountable.

Random drug & alcohol testing

Unannounced screenings are administered routinely to protect the sobriety of the house.

Meeting attendance

Residents attend a required number of recovery meetings each week and maintain contact with a sponsor or mentor.

Employment or productive activity

Residents are expected to work, actively job search, attend school, or volunteer within an agreed timeframe.

Household responsibilities

Residents share chore rotations and keep shared and private spaces clean and respectful.

Guest & visitor policy

Visitors are permitted during approved hours; overnight guests are not allowed for residents' first phase.

Respect & conduct

Violence, threats, theft, or harassment are grounds for immediate discharge from the home.
